1. What Is Ebola?

Ebola Virus Disease (EVD) is a severe, potentially fatal illness in humans. It progresses rapidly and requires strict medical protocol and isolation.

Mode of Transmission

Transmitted through direct contact with blood, body fluids (vomit, diarrhea, sweat, saliva), or contaminated materials of an infected person or deceased person.

2. Common Signs and Symptoms

Onset of symptoms occurs after incubation. Early identification is vital to prevent transmission. Check any symptoms observed below for awareness.

Sudden Onset Fever

High body temperature starting abruptly without prior warning.

Severe Headache

Intense head pain along with debilitating muscle aches.

Weakness & Fatigue

Profound physical exhaustion and body weakness.

Sore Throat

Throat discomfort, difficulty swallowing, or inflammation.

Vomiting & Diarrhoea

Gastrointestinal distress leading to fluid loss.

Abdominal Pain

Severe stomach cramps and persistent abdominal discomfort.

Unexplained Bleeding

Critical Sign

In some severe cases, unexplained bruising, bleeding gums, nosebleeds, or internal gastrointestinal bleeding may occur.

3. Key Infection Prevention & Control (IPC) Measures

Strict adherence to IPC protocols prevents healthcare worker and community transmission.

A

Hand Hygiene

  • Wash hands thoroughly with soap and running water, or use an appropriate alcohol-based hand rub when hands are not visibly soiled.
  • Perform hand hygiene before and after patient contact and immediately after removing medical gloves.
IPC SUBSECTION A Essential Standard
B

Personal Protective Equipment

  • Use appropriate PPE strictly based on the evaluated level of exposure risk.
  • PPE includes: gloves, fluid-resistant gowns/coveralls, masks, eye protection (goggles/face shield), and appropriate footwear.
  • Trained personnel must rigorously follow strict donning and doffing (putting on/removing) procedures.
IPC SUBSECTION B Strict Safety Barrier
C

Isolation & Patient Care

  • Immediately isolate suspected cases in a designated, segregated area.
  • Limit patient contact exclusively to essential, fully trained personnel.
  • Avoid all direct contact with blood, vomit, stool, and other body fluids.
  • Notify appropriate public health authorities immediately.
IPC SUBSECTION C Immediate Action
D

Environmental Cleaning

  • Clean and disinfect contaminated surfaces using approved hospital-grade products and protocols.
  • Handle contaminated linen, medical equipment, and waste safely according to IPC guidelines.
  • Avoid unnecessary handling of contaminated materials.
IPC SUBSECTION D Sanitation Protocol
E

Sharps & Waste Safety

  • Never recap used needles under any circumstance.
  • Dispose of sharps immediately into rigid, puncture-resistant containers.
  • Segregate and dispose of biohazardous waste according to approved safety protocols.
IPC SUBSECTION E Biohazard Safety
F

Safe & Dignified Burial

  • Only trained and authorized burial teams should handle bodies of individuals who died from suspected or confirmed Ebola.
  • Strictly follow approved safe and dignified burial procedures to honor deceased while stopping community transmission.
IPC SUBSECTION F Community Defense
Emergency Protocol

4. What to Do If Ebola Is Suspected

Follow this mandatory 5-step response sequence immediately upon identifying potential symptoms.

1

Avoid Fluids Contact

Avoid direct contact with the suspected patient's body fluids, saliva, blood, vomit, or soiled bedding.

Step 1: Prevention
2

Immediate Separation

Provide immediate separation/isolation in a designated separate room or isolation area.

Step 2: Isolation
3

Alert Authorities

Alert the designated IPC lead and public health authorities immediately via emergency numbers.

Step 3: Notification
4

Safe Testing & Referral

Arrange safe assessment, testing, and transport/referral through authorized health services.

Step 4: Care & Test
5

Record Exposures

Record and assess potential exposures for appropriate follow-up, contact tracing, and monitoring.

Step 5: Contact Tracing

Suspected Ebola Case Action Plan

Follow these 5 immediate steps

  1. 1. Avoid Direct Contact Do not touch blood, vomit, stool, or other body fluids of the individual.
  2. 2. Provide Immediate Isolation Move the person safely to a separate, private isolation room.
  3. 3. Alert IPC Lead & Health Authorities Immediately notify public health services and IPC leads.
  4. 4. Safe Assessment & Referral Allow only trained personnel to conduct testing and transport.
  5. 5. Contact Tracing Log List all individuals who came into contact with the patient.
